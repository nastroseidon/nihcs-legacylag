## ✅ BUILT: The "Pogo" Achievement (12 flavor variants)

**Trigger:** Complete any single level while hopping the whole way — i.e., never move
left/right while standing on the ground (hold jump the entire level). "Lenient" rule:
landing between hops is fine; walking is not.
**Frequency:** Once per session.
**Badge (High Scores):** 🦘 (same badge for all 12 variants)
**Behavior:** When earned, the game randomly displays ONE of the 12 variants below.

The on-screen header for all of them is: **NEW ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED!**

---

### Variant 1
**Title:** 1978 Pogo Stick Champion of Lower Sheboygan, Wisconsin

**Body:**
Congratulations!

After 48 years, three lawsuits, one congressional hearing that accomplished absolutely nothing, and an independent investigation led by a retired mall security guard, the results of the 1978 Lower Sheboygan Pogo Stick Championship have finally been corrected.

The committee determined that Kelly "The Kangaroo" Kapowski secured her victory through an aggressive campaign of distributing free burgers and shakes from The Max to the judging panel. While technically delicious, this was later ruled to be "extremely unsportsmanlike."

Her title has been revoked. It now belongs to you.

Take a moment to appreciate this historic achievement. Thousands trained. Dozens competed. One person finally received the trophy... almost half a century late.

Or...

You completed an entire level by hopping the whole way instead of walking like a reasonably efficient healthcare professional. Dr. Marion Wayne would like to remind you that knees are not a renewable resource.

**Reward:** Eternal bragging rights, imaginary trophy, and the unquestionable privilege of adding "1978 Lower Sheboygan Pogo Stick Champion" to your résumé. Human Resources cannot verify this accomplishment, but they also can't prove it didn't happen.

### Variant 2
**Title:** 1983 Consecutive Bunny Hop Endurance Champion of Kalamazoo, Michigan

**Body:**
Congratulations!

Following a 17-year audit, two hastily assembled review boards, and the accidental discovery of an unlabeled VHS tape behind a vending machine at the Kalamazoo Civic Center, officials have officially overturned the results of the 1983 Consecutive Bunny Hop Endurance Championship.

The investigation concluded that Steve Urkel had unknowingly competed while wearing prototype orthopedic shoes containing experimental industrial-grade springs. Although the manufacturer marketed them as "business casual," the rulebook was later amended to classify them as "unfairly bouncy."

His title has been revoked. It now belongs to you.

History rarely remembers those who finish second. Fortunately for you, history occasionally loses its paperwork for several decades.

Or...

You completed an entire level by jumping from beginning to end without allowing Dr. Marion Wayne the luxury of taking a single normal step. Dr. Marion Wayne would like to point out that gravity is supposed to be an occasional inconvenience, not a lifestyle.

**Reward:** Lifetime membership in the Kalamazoo Association of Competitive Hoppers, one imaginary championship belt, and the satisfaction of knowing your knees are now officially someone else's problem.

### Variant 3
**Title:** 1991 Grand Champion of the Floor-Is-Lava Invitational of Oconomowoc, Wisconsin

**Body:**
Congratulations!

After the original scorecards were rediscovered inside a library book that had been overdue since the Clinton administration, the International Committee for Extremely Questionable Sports convened an emergency session to review the 1991 Floor-Is-Lava Invitational.

His championship has been rescinded. It now belongs to you.

Justice moves slowly, but apparently not as slowly as municipal library systems.

Or...

You refused to let Dr. Marion Wayne touch the ground for an entire level because apparently walking has become optional. Dr. Marion Wayne would like everyone to know he fully supports exercise. This was not exercise.

**Reward:** An honorary lava-proof certificate, a commemorative trophy of questionable historical significance, and bragging rights that absolutely nobody can disprove.

Congratulations!

### Variant 1
**Badge:** 🕊️
**Title:** MANDATORY CONFLICT AVOIDANCE TRAINING: COMPLETED

**Body:**
Congratulations!

You have successfully completed Levels 1–6 without stomping a single enemy.

Not one.

There were creatures wandering directly in your path. Several were openly hostile. At least two of them had facial expressions that strongly suggested they had already imagined your funeral.

And yet, somehow, **every single one of them is still alive.**

The Department of Unnecessarily Aggressive Solutions has reviewed your performance and classified it as:

**"Disturbingly reasonable."**

This is unprecedented.

A special review panel was immediately assembled to determine whether you misunderstood the controls, suffered a prolonged moral crisis, or simply refused to participate in the game's clearly established ecosystem of casual workplace violence.

After fourteen hours of deliberation, the panel reached no conclusion.

One member called your restraint "inspiring."

That individual has been removed from the committee.

**Dr. Marion Wayne**, meanwhile, has completed the required Conflict De-Escalation documentation and would like it entered into the record that at no point did he kick, crush, flatten, concuss, compress, or otherwise dramatically rearrange another living creature.

This may be the cleanest personnel file he has ever had.

Unfortunately, several enemies have now interpreted your mercy as weakness and are forming a committee.

**Reward:** One imaginary white dove, a certificate recognizing your commitment to nonviolence, and the eternal satisfaction of knowing that dozens of hostile creatures went home safely to their families.

Some of those families are probably planning revenge.

Congratulations!

You completed Levels 1–6 without dying.

Not once.

Enemies.

Bad decisions.

Congratulations!

You collected every medallion across all six levels.

Every.

Single.

One.

**"I know it's in there somewhere."**

**"Functionally unrecoverable."**

**"We stopped looking and assumed everyone who remembered where it was would eventually retire."**

IT is deeply uncomfortable.

**You are now the person who knows where everything is.**

People you have never met will begin emailing you shortly.

You collected every medallion across all six levels.

All of them.

The obvious ones.

The inconvenient ones.

The ones hidden just far enough out of the way to make a normal person say:

Repeatedly.

**0 ITEMS MISSING.**
