## ✅ BUILT: The "Pogo" Achievement (12 flavor variants)

**Trigger:** Complete any single level while hopping the whole way — i.e., never move
left/right while standing on the ground (hold jump the entire level). "Lenient" rule:
landing between hops is fine; walking is not.
**Frequency:** Once per session.
**Badge (High Scores):** 🦘 (same badge for all 12 variants)
**Behavior:** When earned, the game randomly displays ONE of the 12 variants below.

The on-screen header for all of them is: **NEW ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED!**

---

### Variant 1
**Title:** 1978 Pogo Stick Champion of Lower Sheboygan, Wisconsin

**Body:**
Congratulations!

After 48 years, three lawsuits, one congressional hearing that accomplished absolutely nothing, and an independent investigation led by a retired mall security guard, the results of the 1978 Lower Sheboygan Pogo Stick Championship have finally been corrected.

The committee determined that Kelly "The Kangaroo" Kapowski secured her victory through an aggressive campaign of distributing free burgers and shakes from The Max to the judging panel. While technically delicious, this was later ruled to be "extremely unsportsmanlike."

Her title has been revoked. It now belongs to you.

Take a moment to appreciate this historic achievement. Thousands trained. Dozens competed. One person finally received the trophy... almost half a century late.

Or...

You completed an entire level by hopping the whole way instead of walking like a reasonably efficient healthcare professional. Dr. Marion Wayne would like to remind you that knees are not a renewable resource.

**Reward:** Eternal bragging rights, imaginary trophy, and the unquestionable privilege of adding "1978 Lower Sheboygan Pogo Stick Champion" to your résumé. Human Resources cannot verify this accomplishment, but they also can't prove it didn't happen.

### Variant 2
**Title:** 1983 Consecutive Bunny Hop Endurance Champion of Kalamazoo, Michigan

**Body:**
Congratulations!

Following a 17-year audit, two hastily assembled review boards, and the accidental discovery of an unlabeled VHS tape behind a vending machine at the Kalamazoo Civic Center, officials have officially overturned the results of the 1983 Consecutive Bunny Hop Endurance Championship.

The investigation concluded that Steve Urkel had unknowingly competed while wearing prototype orthopedic shoes containing experimental industrial-grade springs. Although the manufacturer marketed them as "business casual," the rulebook was later amended to classify them as "unfairly bouncy."

His title has been revoked. It now belongs to you.

History rarely remembers those who finish second. Fortunately for you, history occasionally loses its paperwork for several decades.

Or...

You completed an entire level by jumping from beginning to end without allowing Dr. Marion Wayne the luxury of taking a single normal step. Dr. Marion Wayne would like to point out that gravity is supposed to be an occasional inconvenience, not a lifestyle.

**Reward:** Lifetime membership in the Kalamazoo Association of Competitive Hoppers, one imaginary championship belt, and the satisfaction of knowing your knees are now officially someone else's problem.

### Variant 3
**Title:** 1991 Grand Champion of the Floor-Is-Lava Invitational of Oconomowoc, Wisconsin

**Body:**
Congratulations!

After the original scorecards were rediscovered inside a library book that had been overdue since the Clinton administration, the International Committee for Extremely Questionable Sports convened an emergency session to review the 1991 Floor-Is-Lava Invitational.

Their findings were... troubling.

The committee determined that Joey Gladstone had repeatedly requested "bathroom breaks" during the competition, only to re-enter the course several feet farther along each time. While undeniably creative, this strategy was eventually categorized as "advanced teleportation," which violated at least three paragraphs of the official rules.

His championship has been rescinded. It now belongs to you.

Justice moves slowly, but apparently not as slowly as municipal library systems.

Or...

You refused to let Dr. Marion Wayne touch the ground for an entire level because apparently walking has become optional. Dr. Marion Wayne would like everyone to know he fully supports exercise. This was not exercise.

**Reward:** An honorary lava-proof certificate, a commemorative trophy of questionable historical significance, and bragging rights that absolutely nobody can disprove.

### Variant 4
**Title:** 1987 Vertical Persistence Cup of Walla Walla, Washington

**Body:**
Congratulations!

The results of the 1987 Vertical Persistence Cup have finally been corrected following testimony from four eyewitnesses, a retired scorekeeper, and one remarkably detailed diary kept by a concession stand employee.

Investigators determined that Carlton Banks spent nearly one-third of the competition stopping to admire his own footwork after every successful jump. While judges originally interpreted this as confidence, the official rulebook clearly specifies that contestants must continue hopping "without excessive celebration."

After thirty-nine years, the record has finally been amended. The championship is now yours.

Enjoy the moment. These opportunities don't come around often, mostly because they require decades of administrative delays.

Or...

You completed an entire level by hopping nonstop, forcing Dr. Marion Wayne to reconsider several life choices. He'd also appreciate it if your next accomplishment involved walking. Just once.

**Reward:** A personalized participation ribbon that has somehow become a championship ribbon, one invisible trophy, and permission to mention this accomplishment during annual performance reviews. Human Resources will pretend not to hear you.

### Variant 5
**Title:** 1985 Perpetual Motion Trophy of Ho-Ho-Kus, New Jersey

**Body:**
Congratulations!

After years of speculation, the Office of Retroactive Athletic Corrections has completed its review of the 1985 Ho-Ho-Kus Perpetual Motion Tournament.

Officials discovered that Balki Bartokomous had interpreted the phrase "continuous hopping" to include performing the Dance of Joy between jumps. While the judges initially applauded his enthusiasm, subsequent review determined that choreography and athletic competition occupy two very different sections of the rulebook.

His championship has officially been vacated. It now belongs to you.

Sometimes greatness isn't about being the best. Sometimes it's about waiting forty years for someone else to read the fine print.

Or...

You spent an entire level jumping without ever letting Dr. Marion Wayne experience the simple pleasure of walking like a normal mascot. He's beginning to suspect you enjoy this.

**Reward:** One commemorative plaque, an honorary title nobody asked for, and exclusive membership in a club that almost certainly doesn't exist.

### Variant 6
**Title:** 1990 Elastic Kneecap Invitational of Peculiar, Missouri

**Body:**
Congratulations!

The 1990 Elastic Kneecap Invitational has been under review ever since a local newspaper published an article titled "Did We Measure That Correctly?" Although widely dismissed at the time, the question refused to go away.

A recent forensic analysis of the original measuring equipment determined that Cosmo Kramer had unknowingly competed on a practice course that was approximately 18% shorter than regulation length.

While undeniably energetic, his performance no longer qualifies for the record books. The title has officially been transferred to you.

Sometimes history isn't rewritten by heroes. Sometimes it's rewritten by surveyors with better tape measures.

Or...

You completed an entire level while hopping continuously because apparently the ground had personally offended you. Dr. Marion Wayne would like to remind everyone that he possesses legs for reasons beyond decoration.

**Reward:** A regulation-sized imaginary trophy, unlimited storytelling privileges, and one exceptionally impressive line for your résumé.

### Variant 7
**Title:** 1988 Order of Relentless Boinging of Truth or Consequences, New Mexico

**Body:**
Congratulations!

For nearly four decades, the Order of Relentless Boinging maintained that the 1988 championship results were beyond question. They were mistaken.

Following an anonymous tip, investigators uncovered an administrative error revealing that Norm Peterson had accidentally been credited with an additional 2,000 hops after a scorekeeper spilled root beer onto the official tally sheet. Rather than admit the mistake, the committee simply rounded up and hoped no one would notice.

Eventually... someone noticed.

The championship has now been awarded to its rightful recipient. You.

Take pride in this achievement. Very few people receive a trophy that should have been delivered before the invention of the internet.

Or...

You completed an entire level by jumping nonstop and somehow convinced Dr. Marion Wayne that touching the ground was no longer part of his job description. He's requesting a brief meeting with your space bar.

**Reward:** A beautifully engraved trophy that exists entirely in your imagination, eternal bragging rights, and the unquestionable honor of becoming the official 1988 Order of Relentless Boinging Champion of Truth or Consequences, New Mexico.

### Variant 8
**Title:** 1986 Continuous Boing Cup of Mukwonago, Wisconsin

**Body:**
Congratulations!

For nearly forty years, the official results of the 1986 Continuous Boing Cup remained unchallenged. That changed when a documentary crew filming a completely unrelated special on municipal water towers accidentally uncovered the original judging notes tucked inside a folding chair.

The findings were conclusive.

The committee determined that Danny Tanner paused every twelve jumps to stop and straighten the lane markers because they "looked a little crooked." While his commitment to cleanliness was universally admired, the official rules clearly state that the clock continues to run during unsolicited housekeeping.

His championship has been vacated. It now belongs to you.

Justice may not be swift, but it is surprisingly thorough once enough retirees have free afternoons.

Or...

You completed an entire level by hopping from beginning to end, denying Dr. Marion Wayne the opportunity to enjoy something as basic as walking. He respects your determination. He does not respect what it has done to his knees.

**Reward:** One commemorative championship sash, an invisible trophy polished to imaginary perfection, and the unquestionable privilege of telling everyone you peaked in Mukwonago.

### Variant 9
**Title:** 1992 Grand Champion of the Airborne Appreciation Festival of Boring, Oregon

**Body:**
Congratulations!

Following years of rumors, speculation, and an unfortunate incident involving a mislabeled filing cabinet, the Bureau of Historical Athletic Corrections has officially completed its review of the 1992 Airborne Appreciation Festival.

Their investigation revealed that Uncle Jesse Katsopolis had hired a fog machine for what he described as "dramatic entrances." Unfortunately, the resulting smoke prevented judges from verifying whether he had actually completed several sections of the course.

While undeniably stylish, invisible athleticism is difficult to score.

His title has been revoked. It now belongs to you.

History remembers champions. Paperwork remembers everyone else.

Or...

You completed an entire level without allowing Dr. Marion Wayne to touch the ground for even a moment. He would like to remind you that hopping is generally considered an occasional activity, not a transportation strategy.

**Reward:** An honorary key to the City of Boring (which opens absolutely nothing), a gold-plated imaginary medal, and lifelong bragging rights.

### Variant 10
**Title:** 1984 National Spring-Heel Invitational of Normal, Illinois

**Body:**
Congratulations!

The results of the 1984 National Spring-Heel Invitational have officially been overturned after accountants performing a routine audit discovered that the championship bracket had been printed upside down.

Several phone calls, two independent reviews, and one very awkward apology later, officials confirmed that Cliff Clavin had advanced through the tournament despite technically losing his opening match.

When asked for comment, Cliff insisted the reversal was "part of a little-known postal regulation." It was not.

His championship has been rescinded. It now belongs to you.

Sometimes destiny is written by the victors. Sometimes it's written by whoever finally notices the spreadsheet is backwards.

Or...

You bounced through an entire level because walking was apparently beneath you. Dr. Marion Wayne has begun researching ergonomic pogo sticks.

**Reward:** A framed certificate of improbable excellence, one invisible championship trophy, and the satisfaction of knowing bureaucracy occasionally gets something right... eventually.

### Variant 11
**Title:** 1989 Golden Kangaroo Trophy of Ding Dong, Texas

**Body:**
Congratulations!

After thirty-seven years, the Ding Dong Historical Society finally agreed to digitize its athletic archives. During the process, volunteers discovered an envelope labeled "DO NOT OPEN UNTIL EVERYONE HAS CALMED DOWN."

Inside was the official protest against Kimmy Gibbler, who had reportedly distracted every other competitor by enthusiastically cheering for all of them at the exact same time.

Although the rulebook encourages sportsmanship, it specifically prohibits "weaponized encouragement."

The protest should have been reviewed in 1989. Better late than never.

Her title has been revoked. It now belongs to you.

Very few people receive recognition for an event that predates online banking.

Or...

You completed an entire level by jumping continuously, leaving Dr. Marion Wayne wondering if the floor had somehow become lava without informing him. It hadn't. You were simply committed to the bit.

**Reward:** One spectacularly unnecessary championship trophy, unlimited storytelling rights, and the eternal admiration of absolutely no licensed orthopedic professionals.

### Variant 12
**Title:** 1993 Why, Arizona Invitational for Exceptional Vertical Decision-Making

**Body:**
Congratulations!

For decades, historians believed the results of the 1993 Invitational were beyond dispute. That assumption changed when a former scorekeeper admitted he had accidentally been using the judging rubric from a regional pie-baking competition.

The revised calculations painted a very different picture.

Officials concluded that George Costanza had received bonus points for "excellent crust consistency," a category that, while important elsewhere, had no measurable impact on competitive jumping.

Following an emergency session of the International Committee for Extremely Questionable Sports, his championship has officially been nullified. It now belongs to you.

The committee regrets the inconvenience. George regrets answering his phone.

Or...

You completed an entire level while hopping nonstop, forcing Dr. Marion Wayne to question whether you understand the purpose of legs. He'd like to assure everyone that walking remains fully supported by modern medicine.

**Reward:** A championship jacket that exists only in spirit, one beautifully engraved imaginary trophy, and the unquestionable distinction of becoming the 1993 Invitational Champion of Why, Arizona. It's a title nobody can take away... mostly because nobody else wants it.
